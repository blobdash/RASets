SYSTEM LOG MDAT-0001: SECURITY

The security system consists of an automated
network of robotic sentries. These sentries will
eliminate any threats encountered outside of the
specimen containment apparatus, or within it, if
the actions of a subject are deemed to be a threat.



SYSTEM LOG MDAT-0002: MAINTENANCE

The security system is overseen by a series of
military-grade Guardian Defense Sentries.
Each is equipped with advanced artificial
intelligence and has been hardwired into the
facility to govern portions of the production
process and its underlying systems, making the
GD Series invaluable to ongoing operations.

SYSTEM LOG MDAT-0003: HARVESTING

Subjects are extracted at a rate of 100
individuals per Earth rotation. Subjects are
transferred directly to the facility's holding
tanks via a precision particle beam that
deconstructs the subject and reconstructs it
on site. Success rate is 99.999%.


SYSTEM LOG MDAT-0004: INITIAL PROCESSING

The subject is first stripped of clothing and
accoutrements then purged of hair and non-living
dermis. The subject is then equipped with a
Sensory Stimulator and placed in amniotic fluid.
Following initial processing, the subject is
transferred to cold storage.


SYSTEM LOG MDAT-0005: COLD STORAGE

Temperature is decreased, lowering the
subject's resistance to foreign elements. The
duo-isotope is then introduced into the tank
and slowly absorbed into the subject's body
through the lungs. Subject may experience
physical distress at this stage.


SYSTEM LOG MDAT-0006: EUPHORIC IMPRINTING

Euphoric imprinting is initiated once the subject
has ingested the duo-isotope. Using the Sensory
Stimulator, the subject is first cleansed of
previous mental printing, causing it to revert to
an infantile state, then inundated with sensations
designed to increase serotonin production.


SYSTEM LOG MDAT-0007: EXTRACTION PHASE 1

Serotonin is drained from the subject's brain
through an extraction spine. The Sensory
Stimulator then produces negative sensations,
increasing adrenaline synthesis. Ferritin is
extracted from the liver, causing shock and
loss of neural function in 60% of cases.


SYSTEM LOG MDAT-0008: EXTRACTION PHASE 2

The adrenal glands are drained at the peak of
adrenaline production. Extraction destroys
the internal organs, and the mortality rate
at this stage is 1,000 per 1,000. The remains
are dried, crushed, and processed for use
in cosmetics and other products.

SYSTEM LOG MDAT-0009: PSYCHOTROPIC VIRUS

Serotonin, adrenaline, and ferritin are irradiated,
combined and flash-fused. Heightened pH levels
catalyze a toxic change in the adrenaline. The
resulting compound is infused with molecular oxygen,
causing the ferritin to oxidize. The mixture becomes
a blue-colored liquid viral suspension. This is
the psychotropic virus in its pure form.

HISTORY MDAT-0001: THE MOON

Due to its location and its uninhabitable
environment, the Moon provides an ideal location
for the main processing center. The facility
operates entirely below the crust and is
protected from scanning devices, minimizing
the risk of detection by any means but direct
physical excavation.

HISTORY MDAT-0002: MEDICINAL PURPOSES

Subjects are placed under physiological and
psychological stress, leading to accelerated
endocrine production and chemical yield.
Chemical extracts form the basis for
products such as aphrodisiacs, pyschotropic
drugs and military-grade psychostimulants.


HISTORY MDAT-0003: THE FACILITIES
In order to maximize chemical output, subjects are
kept in states of mental and physical duress, such
as pain, fear and anxiety, throughout the
extraction process. Staff in other facilities have
occasionally reported experiencing emotional
stress and depression, even when not aware of
any conscious empathy with the subjects. To limit
the risk of these emotional factors affecting
production yield, all high-value extraction
facilities are now unmanned and automated.HISTORY MDAT-0004: IMPROVING OUTPUT

Over time, varying experimental procedures
have been administered in an effort to
maximize chemical yield. Subjects have
occasionally exhibited accelerated
physical evolution, specifically in the
endocrine system.


HISTORY MDAT-0005: ETHICAL CONTROVERSY

Some argue that the subjects are sentient
creatures and not suitable for harvesting.
Opponents insist that their bestial nature
makes them little more than natural resources,
and that the benefits gained from their use
far outweigh any ethical concerns.


HISTORY MDAT-0006: THE PLANET EARTH

Cultivation began during the prehistory of
the species. Living in small, isolated
groups, preoccupied with hunting and foraging,
lacking any advanced technological knowledge,
and tending to superstition, the early humans
were easily cultivated and harvested.


HISTORY MDAT-0007: SELECTION PROCESS

Individuals and small groups in remote areas
are ideal for harvesting.  Occasionally
atmospheric conditions may interfere with
the process, resulting in subjects
rematerializing on Earth, sometimes in
locations far from the origin of transfer.


HISTORY MDAT-0008: MANIPULATION

Since the earliest stages of their evolution,
subversive strategies have been used to control
the species' cultural development. One
tactic has been to introduce contrasting
theologies to the Earth, throwing the
population into factionalism and infighting.


HISTORY MDAT-0009: THREATS TO OPERATION

Unlike species harvested elsewhere, humans
have rapidly developed primitive space
travel and energy weapons technology. This
has necessitated the implementation of
advanced security procedures to ensure the
ongoing viability of harvesting operations.


HISTORY MDAT-0010: EXTERNAL PRESSURES

Information regarding harvest and extraction
procedures has leaked to mainstream media,
resulting in public outcry and interference
from law enforcement. In response, the
security at each facility has been augmented
by military-grade Guardian Defense Sentries.


HISTORY MDAT-0011: CONTACT

One century ago, a group of activists traveled
to Earth in order to make contact with the
subject species. Their vessel became unstable
and crashed in the southwestern North American
continent, and the probability that its crew
survived the incident is negligible.


HISTORY MDAT-0012: HUMAN TECHNOLOGY

The development of human technology, especially
in the areas of physics and engineering, has
progressed at an unexpectedly brisk pace.
Some believe that humans were able to recover
enough material from the crash to understand
and draw on our technology.


VIRAL STATUS MDAT-001: INGREDIENTS

The suspension's components in their unprocessed
forms are lethal, and the host organisms are
themselves classified as a Class X viral vector.
The subjects and their habitat are protected
solely for possible application in the
development of further pharmaceutical products.


VIRAL STATUS MDAT-002: CHARACTERISTICS

The suspension contains a highly potent
viral agent that causes intense extrasensory
experiences when ingested, and can seriously
damage the health of the user if taken in
large doses (See: Side-Effects). Despite
this, use of the suspension is widespread,
and a high percentage of the population is at
present in a state of addiction or withdrawal.
VIRAL STATUS MDAT-003: SIDE EFFECTS

Exposure to this agent causes a paralytic
overload of euphoric feelings, resulting in a
blissful coma-like state. The duration of the
coma is directly proportional to the dosage.
Acute overuse invariably results in death.
For these reasons, the suspension is a
controlled substance and its production
is prohibited by law (See: Ingredients).
VIRAL STATUS MDAT-004: LEGAL STATUS

Legal statutes expressly forbid the sale of
pharmaceutical agents produced from non-
consenting advanced humanoids. However,
the incredible effectiveness of such products
continues to fuel an overwhelming demand,
and they are easily available through a
vibrant black market.

VIRAL STATUS MDAT-005: LEGALIZATION

Critics claim anti-cruelty measures would be
served by legalizing the products, subjecting
production to public audit, and offering
economic incentives for humane treatment.
This is opposed both by criminal elements who
depend on the black market for income and by
conservative political groups that view the
existence of such products as a moral outrage.
VIRAL STATUS MDAT-006: POLITICAL CONCERNS

Production has increased despite legal
challenges and attention from regulatory
agencies. The Sector Overlord maintains
that the substance has valuable medical
and military applications, using these
needs as political capital to divert
attention from our other operations.
(See: Additional Information)
VIRAL STATUS MDAT-007: ADDITIONAL INFO.
The current Sector Overlord took control of viral 
and medicinal production upon taking the reins of 
power. Although he maintains a private militia 
equal to that of many small nations, he was able 
to gain control over the organization without the 
use of force by political maneuvering alone. His 
military strength and political shrewdness, 
combined with sheer ruthlessness, have helped 
him to survive several attempted coups.
VIRAL STATUS MDAT-007: ADDITIONAL INFO

Though illegal, the virus is reportedly
popular among the higher political circles.
It is rumored that the Sector Overlord
supplies politicians with the virus in
order to curry favor and keep law
enforcement agencies away from his operations.
